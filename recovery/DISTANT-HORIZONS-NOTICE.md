# Distant Horizons notice for this archive

The source-recovery archive in this folder holds the older 039 engine's work. Among its review evidence are 62 `javap` listings of Distant Horizons classes (paths containing `dh-bytecode/com.seibel.distanthorizons.*`), made while checking how that engine's optional backend called Distant Horizons.
