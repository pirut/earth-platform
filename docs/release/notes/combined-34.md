Earth Platform build 34: everything in one jar.

**Play in a fresh world.** Give the game about 6 GB of memory with Photon.

Needs Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge 21.1.256 or newer. For shaders, add Iris 1.8 and Sodium 0.8 and the stock Photon v1.3b pack.

New since build 33:
- Creative travel map: `/earth map` opens the globe; pick a place and fly there.
- The old Distant Horizons support is gone, so the jar is about 5 MB. Distant Horizons can be installed alongside and pauses in Earth worlds.
- Faster loading: teleport once near data is in, cheaper coastal far terrain, US elevation decoded once.
- The Earth data cache is capped (8 GB by default, `cacheLimitGb`) and trimmed by last use.
- NeoForge 21.1.256 or newer is accepted.

Known: dark far slabs can show in the Amazon for the first ~20 seconds; Everglades far water is still patchy.
