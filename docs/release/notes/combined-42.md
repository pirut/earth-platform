Earth Platform build 42: Chunky support, sharper Japan, France and Canada, other mods' trees and structures, and softer biome edges.

**Play in a fresh world.** Give the game about 6 GB of memory with Photon.

Needs Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge 21.1.256 or newer. For shaders, add Iris 1.8 and Sodium 0.8 and the stock Photon v1.3b pack.

New since build 41:
- Chunky can pre-generate Earth worlds. Earth worlds now save the ground they generate around the surface, like vanilla saves every chunk it makes; untouched deep rock and open sky are still rebuilt on demand.
- Detailed national elevation in Japan (GSI), France (IGN RGE ALTI) and Canada (NRCan HRDEM), picked per area and downloaded only where you play.
- Trees and structures that other mods add (biome modifiers, data packs) now generate in Earth worlds.
- Softer, ragged borders between biomes and ground types, so places like Miami Beach no longer show rectangles of sand and marsh.

Known: dark far slabs can show in the Amazon for the first ~20 seconds; Everglades far water is still patchy.
