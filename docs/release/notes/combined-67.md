Earth Platform build 67: Earth Platform now runs on Fabric too, Axiom and WorldEdit work in the Earth world, and Antarctica's mountains and ice stand at their real height up close again.

Needs Minecraft 1.21.1 and either NeoForge 21.1.256 or newer, or Fabric Loader 0.16.14 or newer with Fabric API. For shaders, add Iris 1.8 and Sodium 0.8 and a Voxy-capable pack such as Photon v1.3b. Give the game about 6 GB of memory with shaders.

New since build 66:
- Earth Platform now has a Fabric build, `earthplatform-fabric-*.jar`, for Minecraft 1.21.1 with Fabric API. It is the same mod: the same terrain, far terrain, jet stream, map and server, and the same look with Sodium, Iris and a Voxy-capable shader pack. It also loads on Quilt. Use the NeoForge jar on NeoForge and the Fabric jar on Fabric; a server and its players need the same one.
- Axiom works in the Earth world on the Fabric build. Axiom reads and writes the 16-block carrier under each column, so in an Earth world it saw nothing to aim at and painted nowhere. Its tools now see the real ground at any height, and brush strokes and undo land in the world and reach every player on a server. Tested with Axiom 6.1.3.
- Antarctica's ground near you stands at its real height again. Build 66's new sea cliffs took every place the land cover map has no data for as the sea, and that map stops at 60° S, so all of Antarctica counted as coast and the ground near you was cut down to an icy cove at sea level, under far terrain still standing at its real height. `/earth goto` to Vinson Massif landed you at Y 91, inside what looked like the ice sheet, instead of on the ice at about 4,950 m. Places the land cover map leaves out now count as sea only where the elevation data has them at or under sea level, so Vinson's top is at Y 4,947 again and travel lands you on it. Ground in Antarctica generated with build 66 keeps its sea-level coves; ground generated from now on is right.
- Mount Fuji's summit no longer has a hole with pillars standing in it. The surface water record sees shadows, melt and late snow on bare mountain tops, and the river map traced them across Fuji's crater rim as ragged pools; a river takes the lowest ground round it as its level, which there lay far down the slope, so the pools sank into pits. Rivers no longer run over summits and ridge tops, where the ground falls away on nearly every side. Rivers in valleys, as in Iceland, Scotland, Yosemite and New Zealand, are unchanged.
- Perito Moreno's ice front meets the lake again. Water beside a glacier counted as more of the ice when the ice outnumbered the land round it, so the lake's edge under the front turned to ice and the cliff stood back from a beach; water now has to be outnumbered by the ice too.
- Glaciers no longer sink into straight-edged steps and trenches. The elevation data lost much of the ice in 2000 and filled the gaps in smooth dips tens of metres under the ice, which crossed Perito Moreno's tongue as a zigzag crack twenty blocks deep. On snow and ice the Copernicus correction now also lifts ground that lies under Copernicus. New worlds only; existing worlds keep their ground.
- Glacier tongues below the snowline are bare ice: pale blue, deep blue where cleanest, with patches of old snow, instead of one flat white. Snowfields and ice sheets above the snowline, and all of Antarctica's interior, stay snow.
- WorldEdit works in the Earth world, at any height and anywhere on the planet. Its NeoForge build read and wrote only the 16-block carrier under each column, so selections, `//set`, `//paste` and `//undo` failed or did nothing outside Y 0 to 15, and its undo history stopped at Y 2,047. They now reach the real ground, edits far from any player load that ground first, `//setbiome` changes the Earth's biomes, and the far terrain shows big builds and undos once that ground is saved, usually within a minute. `//regen` is refused for now rather than wiping the area. A 2 million block `//set` takes about 10 seconds. This WorldEdit fix is in the NeoForge build; on Fabric, use Axiom for now. FastAsyncWorldEdit runs only on Paper-style servers, not on NeoForge or Fabric. Thanks to @Tkda-h1m on YouTube for asking.
