Earth Platform build 75 grows forests from a real map of the world's forest types.

Requires Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge 21.1.256 or newer, or Fabric Loader 0.16.14 or newer with Fabric API. Optional mods may require newer loader versions.

### Terrain

- New Earth worlds choose each forest's trees from the Copernicus 100 m forest type map. It shows where forests are needleleaf or broadleaf, and where the broadleaf is evergreen or deciduous. Conifer and broadleaf woods stand in patches a few hundred meters across, as much of each as the map shows. The climate still decides how they grow.
- What changes: the Black Forest becomes dark spruce instead of birch and oak. Japan's mountains are mostly conifer. The American Southeast and the Mediterranean get pinewoods among their oaks. Southern Siberia and Finland gain birch woods. Central India's monsoon forests replace dark oak.
- Only vanilla biomes and trees are used. The map ships inside the mod, so nothing extra is downloaded while you play.
- Existing worlds keep their forests. Turn it off for new worlds with the "Forest types from map" setting or `-Dearthplatform.earth.forests=false`.

Forest types: Copernicus Global Land Service, Land Cover 100 m collection 3, epoch 2019 (Buchhorn et al. 2020), CC BY 4.0.
