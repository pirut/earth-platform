Earth Platform build 37: the River Cam and other small streams.

**Play in a fresh world.** Give the game about 6 GB of memory with Photon.

Needs Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge 21.1.256 or newer. For shaders, add Iris 1.8 and Sodium 0.8 and the stock Photon v1.3b pack.

New since build 36:
- Streams too narrow for the satellite water map are traced from the valleys in the elevation data, where the rainfall supports them. The River Cam now runs through Cambridge and under Clare Bridge, and small creeks appear in valleys elsewhere. Flat fens get no made-up rivers.
- Streams use only data the mod already reads, and teleports are as fast as in build 36.
- New worlds only: existing worlds keep the rivers they were made with.

Known: dark far slabs can show in the Amazon for the first ~20 seconds; Everglades far water is still patchy.
