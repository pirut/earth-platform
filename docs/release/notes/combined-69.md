Earth Platform build 69: ComputerCraft works in the Earth world. Turtles move at any height, GPS finds you again, and on Fabric computers see the modems and drives beside them.

Needs Minecraft 1.21.1 and either NeoForge 21.1.256 or newer, or Fabric Loader 0.16.14 or newer with Fabric API. For shaders, add Iris 1.8 and Sodium 0.8 and a Voxy-capable pack such as Photon v1.3b. Give the game about 6 GB of memory with shaders.

New since build 68:
- CC: Tweaked turtles now move in the Earth world, on both the NeoForge and Fabric builds. Turtles checked each move against the 16-block carrier under each column, so above Y 15 or below Y 0 every move failed with "Too high to move" or "Too low to move". They could still dig and place, but they couldn't go anywhere. Now they move anywhere from Y -16,384 to the top of the world at Y 16,383, across cube edges, up, down and through the ground.
- CC: Tweaked wireless modems, rednet and GPS work in the Earth world again. A wireless modem reaches further the higher it sits. CC worked out that range from the 16-block carrier, so above Y 96, which covers most of the Earth's land, the range came out below zero and no message got through: `gps.locate` always failed and rednet over wireless was silent. The range now grows with height as it does in the Overworld, from 64 blocks at Y 96 to the full high-altitude range of 384 blocks at Y 320. Above Y 320 it stays at 384, so on any mountain or above the clouds you get the full range. Ender modems reach any distance, as before.
- On the Fabric build, computers now see blocks placed next to them in the Earth world: modems, disk drives, monitors, printers and speakers. Fabric API remembers what sits next to each block and forgets it when a block entity loads or is removed, but Earth never told it about the block entities in its own storage, so a computer kept seeing the empty space that was there when it started, until the area was reloaded. Earth now sends Fabric's block entity load and unload events. Other Fabric mods that look up energy or item storage next to a block rely on the same events and should benefit too.
- Tested with CC: Tweaked 1.120.2 on NeoForge at Yosemite at Y 1,300, in the sky at Y 6,000, underground at Y -1,000 and at the Y 16,383 ceiling. Computers, turtles (moving, digging, placing), monitors (including a 3x2 wall, which keeps its picture after you rejoin), wired modems and cables across cube edges, disk drives and floppies, printers, speakers, redstone, command computers, pocket computers, GPS hosts 322 blocks away at Y 6,000, and an ender pocket computer getting a GPS fix 5,000 blocks from its hosts all work. On Fabric, computers, turtles, GPS, wired networks with disk drives, monitors and pocket computers were checked at Y 1,300 and Y 6,000. As in the Overworld, computers and turtles only run where a player is near enough to keep the ground loaded.
- WorldEdit's `//regen` now works in the Earth world (NeoForge). It rebuilds the selected area exactly as the Earth generated it: the real ground, rock, water, trees and ores. Use it to undo griefing or a build you don't want any more. `//undo` brings your edit back, and `//regen -b` restores biomes too. Before, it was refused, because WorldEdit would have rebuilt the area from an empty flat world. A 128 by 128 by 128 area takes about 30 seconds on our test server.
- A little less work every tick near the ground. Each tick the game picks about 1,300 random blocks around you to check for effects such as dripping water, torch flames and biome particles like the Nether's ash. In the Earth world, a 16-block cube whose biomes have no particles now skips the slow biome lookup, and the water check reuses the block it already read. In our profile the biome lookups disappeared from that work and it took about half the time. Nothing looks different: biomes with particles still get them.
