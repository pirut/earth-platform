Earth Platform build 43: smoother distant terrain.

**Play in a fresh world.** Give the game about 6 GB of memory with Photon.

Needs Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge 21.1.256 or newer. For shaders, add Iris 1.8 and Sodium 0.8 and the stock Photon v1.3b pack.

New since build 42:
- Far terrain draws the ground as a smooth surface on every far level. The two nearest far levels lay each column's block texture over that surface, and their trees stay as blocks.
- Finer far levels fade in as they arrive instead of popping in.
- Cliffs and peaks above the camera in far terrain no longer vanish or show as thin spikes.

Not yet tested on a Mac. If far terrain looks wrong on macOS, please report it.

Known: dark far slabs can show in the Amazon for the first ~20 seconds; Everglades far water is still patchy.
