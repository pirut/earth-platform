Earth Platform build 48: a softer edge where real blocks meet distant terrain.

**Play in a fresh world.** Give the game about 6 GB of memory with Photon.

Needs Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge 21.1.256 or newer. For shaders, add Iris 1.8 and Sodium 0.8 and the stock Photon v1.3b pack.

New since build 47:
- Under shaders, the small step shadows on real blocks no longer stop at a hard line where distant terrain begins. The distant blocks just past the edge now get the same step shadows, which fade out as the blocks get smaller with distance. Far-off slopes stay smooth and stripe-free.

Not yet tested on a Mac.

Known: dark far slabs can show in the Amazon for the first ~20 seconds; Everglades far water is still patchy.
