Earth Platform build 78 makes arriving somewhere new, and joining for the first time, safe from structure freezes, and makes trips more reliable.

Requires Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge 21.1.256 or newer, or Fabric Loader 0.16.14 or newer with Fabric API. Optional mods may require newer loader versions.

### Structures and servers

- Arriving somewhere or joining for the first time never freezes the server on structures. Structures that keep failing no longer leave holes (#267).

### Travel

- The destination stays loaded while you wait (#267).
- Every trip lands or gives up within about 6 minutes, with a message (#267).
- You never land inside a block (#267).
- Paid travel charges levels only when you actually land (#267).
- Dying or taking a portal cancels a pending trip (#267).

### Releases

- This build is published on CurseForge and Modrinth as Beta, for both the NeoForge and Fabric jars. The version in the Mods screen is `0.1.0-beta.78`.
