Earth Platform build 47: blocky distant terrain, no more vanishing ground while flying.

**Play in a fresh world.** Give the game about 6 GB of memory with Photon.

Needs Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge 21.1.256 or newer. For shaders, add Iris 1.8 and Sodium 0.8 and the stock Photon v1.3b pack.

New since build 46:
- Distant terrain looks like Minecraft blocks again: far blocks are lit face by face like real blocks, easing to smoother light only where a block is a couple of pixels wide, so there are still no stripes under Photon.
- Mixed ground cover in the distance shows as patches instead of salt and pepper.
- While flying, big pieces of the world no longer vanish: blocks that unload stay drawn until the distant terrain covers them, and ground behind you stays loaded instead of downloading again.

Not yet tested on a Mac.

Known: dark far slabs can show in the Amazon for the first ~20 seconds; Everglades far water is still patchy.
