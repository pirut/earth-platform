Earth Platform - Photon v1.3b presets

START WITH: Earth-Photon-Balanced-v1.3b.txt
FALLBACK:   Earth-Photon-Fast-v1.3b.txt

These are settings files, not a shader pack or a replacement mod JAR. Use your
existing latest Earth Platform mod and a separately obtained Photon v1.3b pack.
Photon's official page: https://modrinth.com/shader/photon-shader
Other Photon releases need their option names/ranges revalidated first.

APPLY IN IRIS

1. Select Photon v1.3b and open "Shader Pack Settings...".
2. Export your current settings if you want a backup.
3. Reset Photon settings first so unrelated old Ultra/PBR choices do not remain.
   In the checked Iris version, hold Shift while clicking Reset.
4. Use "Import settings from file" and choose the Balanced TXT file. Apply.
   The profile may read Custom; that is expected for these tuned settings.
5. If needed, reset and import Fast instead. Import only one preset at a time.

WHAT BALANCED CHANGES

- Terrain stays at full rendering resolution. TAA is on; whole-scene temporal
  upscaling (TAAU), motion blur and depth of field are off. CAS sharpening is on.
- Clouds use Photon's temporal upscaling, independent of terrain resolution.
  Earth scale is one metre per block, with cumulus at 2500 m, altocumulus at
  5000 m and cirrus at 7500 m above sea level. Cumulus thickness is reduced to
  0.30 of its altitude, about 750 m, rather than a multi-kilometre dense bank.
  Upper-layer thickness is 0.10. Everest-scale peaks can remain above these.
- Photon's own random/biome weather variations are enabled, with lower cloud
  coverage and density. This does not use the mod's regional cloud-weather code.
- Three regular cloud types stay on. Extra congestus towers and noctilucent
  clouds are off. Cumulus uses 32/16 horizon/zenith steps, 4 lighting steps and
  1 ambient step, versus the checked defaults of 40/20, 6 and 2.
- Shadows use 1536 resolution and 96-block distance. Colored shadows, contact
  ray shadows, entity/block-entity shadows and cloud shadows are off.
- Volumetric fog and sky lighting remain, but fog intensity is reduced to 0.35.
  Extra colored light shafts, voxel colored lighting, screen-space environment
  reflections, water parallax/displacement, caustics, POM and PBR mapping are off.
  Sky reflections and ordinary water appearance remain.

FAST DIFFERENCES

Fast uses 1024-resolution shadows at 80 blocks, disables extra shader AO,
volumetric fog and SH sky lighting, and skips the middle altocumulus layer.
Low clouds use 24/12 primary steps and 3 lighting steps. Terrain resolution,
Minecraft block geometry, TAA, sharpening and the Earth-scale cloud heights stay.

SCOPE AND VERIFICATION

These presets change Photon settings globally for that selected shader pack,
including other worlds, until you reset/import another preset. They explicitly
set cloud scale and height, so Earth Platform respects these chosen values.
Changing the mod's cloudHeight afterward will not override the preset's values.

Photon supplies its own clouds/atmosphere. The mod's custom cloud volumes,
regional weather envelope and plain-renderer curved-terrain path are not used
while a shader pack is active. Settings alone cannot make those pipelines the
same. Existing mod terrain/compatibility handling still applies.

All 66 option names, menu/profile exposure, booleans and enumerated values in
each preset were checked against the pinned Photon v1.3b archive. Files were
read back and the source archive hash was verified unchanged. validation.json
records hashes and the check result. No pack code or active installation was
modified. These presets have NOT been visually tested or benchmarked in-game.

Compare the same loaded location with the same time/weather, resolution and far
setting. Test ground-level movement, mountain flight and spyglass use. A shader
preset cannot by itself remove CPU chunk-generation or mesh-loading bottlenecks.
