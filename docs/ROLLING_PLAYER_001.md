## Native-client validation protocol

1. Capture original Overworld position/mode. `/earth_roam enter` opens ground128; endpoint forms are `enter abyss` (ground−11000) and `enter summit` (ground8849). Wait for READY and `/earth_roam status` showing collision125 and an idle publication. Initial feet Z−1.5 naturally requests a first adjacent negative-Z window after bootstrap.
2. `/earth_roam walk` enables Adventure walking at the current clear position. Feet settle129 (abyss−10999, summit8850). To reproduce the familiar edit ray, position at4.5,feetY,−1.5 yaw0 pitch15. Ordinary right-click places diorite once; left-click removes. Hold/release only emits one intention. Record the durable revision. To test moving away with the edit preserved, walk around its side rather than through the block.
3. Walk at least6–10seconds across several16m cube boundaries, stop and wait for READY, then reverse. `/earth_roam status` captures current center, pending candidate, revision, physical actor counters and the last512 physics ticks. Native/DH coverage must advance without `/resume`, and collision remains125. The original edited cube must still visibly retain its block on return.
4. A diagonal horizontal walk tests simultaneous X/Z changes. `/earth_roam fly`, then ordinary upward/downward flight, tests signed cube-Y window changes. Return to a known clear body and `/earth_roam walk`; it preserves position rather than teleporting to the original fixture.
5. A ray aimed at an original-cube boundary voxel must return IMMUTABLE_BOUNDARY without revision change. Sky/outside-cube/third-person interactions remain rejected. Automated tests also exhaust every shell voxel and delayed/uncooperative generation; delayed-provider GUI behavior is not implied by those tests.
6. Trigger actual F3+T during a pending window, then attempt movement. Rendering closes and collision is fenced while native-height safety remains. `/earth_roam leave`, wait for actor drain, then re-enter. The original cube's durable edit must survive.
7. Save & Quit directly while walking, reopen the SAME world, verify original normal-world location/mode, then re-enter and revisit the edit. Death recovery follows the reviewed full-height flow. Finish with leave, save and close.
